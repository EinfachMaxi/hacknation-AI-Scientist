EXP-8492: Graphene Oxide Sensor Calibration

Hypothesis: Optimizing the thermal reduction temperature to 450°C in an argon atmosphere will increase the sensor's sensitivity to volatile organic compounds (VOCs) by 25% compared to standard chemical reduction methods.

EST. TOTAL TIME: 14h 30m
STEPS: 05
SCIENTIST REVIEW
01

Substrate Preparation

30 min

Clean the SiO2/Si substrates using a standard RCA sequence. Rinse thoroughly with deionized water (18.2 MΩ·cm) and blow dry with N2 gas.

EQUIPMENT
Ultrasonic Bath, Spin Coater, N2 Gun
02

Graphene Oxide Spin Coating

45 min

Dispense 50 µL of 2mg/mL GO aqueous dispersion onto the prepared substrate. Spin at 3000 rpm for 60s to achieve a uniform monolayer.

warning
CRITICAL NOTE Ensure dispersion is completely homogenized before dispensing to prevent aggregate formation which degrades sensor baseline. Include negative control (bare SiO2) for baseline normalization.
03

Thermal Reduction

12 h

Transfer samples to the tube furnace. Purge with Argon (200 sccm) for 30 mins. Ramp temperature to 450°C at 5°C/min. Hold for 2 hours, then allow to naturally cool to room temperature under continuous Argon flow.

psychology KNOWLEDGE GARDEN INSIGHT

Protocol adjusted based on historical data. Previous calibrations using chemical reduction (Hydrazine) showed a 15% variance in baseline resistance over 48 hours.
