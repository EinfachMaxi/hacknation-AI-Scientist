# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Researchers, lab engineers, and scientific founders who need fast, actionable experiment plans. They usually work under time pressure, often in low-distraction environments, and care more about clarity and trust than visual novelty.

## Product Purpose
The product turns a natural-language hypothesis into an executable experiment plan with protocol, materials, budget, timeline, and validation criteria. Success means users can move from idea to lab execution quickly, with enough confidence to act on the generated plan.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, trustworthy.
The UI should feel operational and credible, like a serious scientific tool that reduces uncertainty.

## Anti-references
- Over-stylized SaaS dashboards with heavy gradients and decorative glow
- Marketing-like UI patterns inside core task flows
- Crowded card grids that hide the main action under visual noise

## Design Principles
- Prioritize task clarity over decoration.
- Keep hierarchy obvious, users should understand next actions in seconds.
- Use restrained color, one accent for action and state.
- Keep interaction patterns familiar and consistent across screens.
- Design for sustained use, reduce visual fatigue during long sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast standards for primary UI text and controls. Keep keyboard focus states visible, preserve clear labels for form controls, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate status.
